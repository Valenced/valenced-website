<Callout type="warn" title="Client modifications break Discord's Terms of Service">
    Using Valenced, like any client modification, is against [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms). Use it at your own risk.
</Callout>

To date, Discord has not been known to ban users *solely* for using client modifications. Valenced does not add abusive functionality, does not perform self-bot behavior, and aims to stay indistinguishable from a stock client on the network.

That said:

- **No guarantees.** Discord could change its enforcement policy at any time. The Valenced team is not responsible for anything that happens to your account.
- **Don't use plugins that abuse the API.** Plugins that automate user actions (self-bots, message scrapers, etc.) significantly increase your risk. Such plugins are not allowed in the official plugin repository.
- **Be careful what you install.** Plugins run with full access to your Discord client, including your messages and token. Only install plugins from sources you trust. See [Installing Plugins](/docs/installing-plugins) for more.

## Privacy

Valenced itself blocks several of Discord's tracking and crash-reporting by default (via the built-in **No Track** plugin).  
You can also **opt-in** to anonymous telemetry to help the team improve Valenced for everyone. Collected data can be viewed locally.
